SaveSutra

Privacy notice

Last updated 4 November 2026 · Written with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 in mind. This is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.

Who is responsible

SaveSutra is an open-source project by Manav Yadav. If you run your own SaveSutra server, you are responsible for the data on it.

What is processed

  • The links you save, your notes and any screenshots you attach.
  • Public information about each link: title, caption or description, author, thumbnail, and for videos the audio and on-screen text.
  • If you use the WhatsApp bot: your WhatsApp number and the messages you send it.
  • Settings such as your theme and, if you add one, your AI key (kept on your device).

Why

Only to summarise, organise and search your own saves. SaveSutra has no ads, does no profiling and never sells data.

Where it goes

  • Your device by default. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on one of the options below.
  • Google Gemini API (optional, with your key): the public text and media of the link are sent for analysis. Google’s API terms apply.
  • Your own server (optional): saves are stored in its database. Videos are downloaded only for analysis and deleted straight after; they are never stored or shared.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) (optional): messages to the bot pass through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Consent and your rights

The app asks for your consent before first use. You can access, correct, export (Excel, CSV, JSON) and erase your data at any time in Settings, and withdraw consent the same way: it is as easy as giving it. On WhatsApp, send stop to delete your WhatsApp saves and stop all messages.

Retention

Saves are kept until you delete them. Temporary video files are deleted within minutes. Server logs, if any, should be kept to a minimum by whoever runs the server.

Children

SaveSutra is not meant for children under 18.

Respecting creators and platforms

SaveSutra keeps notes and links, not copies of other people’s content. It only reads public posts, never logs in to any platform, and is meant for personal reference. Respect each platform’s terms and creators’ rights.

Questions and grievances

Open an issue at github.com/GOD-MANAV/savesutra/issues. We aim to reply well within the 90 days the DPDP Rules allow.

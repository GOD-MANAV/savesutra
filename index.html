Three steps. No folders.

01 · SHARE

Share from any app

Tap Share → SaveSutra on Android, paste a link, forward it to the SaveSutra WhatsApp bot, or import years of links from an old WhatsApp chat.

02 · UNDERSTAND

It reads the whole thing

AI reads the caption, transcribes the audio and scans the frames, then writes a summary and picks out the tools, places, links, prices and steps.

03 · FIND

Find it the way you remember it

“AI tool for slides”, “café in Hauz Khas”, “what did I save last week”. Browse as cards, a table, a whiteboard or a timeline.

Built for how India saves things

WhatsApp-first

Keep forwarding links like you always have. The bot saves them and answers “find goa”. Import an exported chat and every old link keeps its date.

Deep extraction

Links said in the audio or shown on screen, not just the caption. Places open in Maps, tools open their site.

Smart collections

“All AI tools”, “Places”, “Books”, “Recipes” build themselves from what’s inside your saves.

Four layouts

Cards to browse, a numbered table to sort and export to Excel, a whiteboard to brainstorm, a timeline with a heatmap.

Search like you remember

Typo-tolerant search across titles, notes, tools and places, with filters like cat:ai and from:youtube.

Weekly digest & resurfacing

See what you saved this week, “On this day” memories, and To try / Done status so good saves don’t die.

Offline-first

Your library lives on your phone. Connect your own server only if you want the WhatsApp bot.

Bring your own AI key

Use a free Gemini key. Without one, smart rules still tag, categorise and extract links.

Phone, browser and WhatsApp

An Android app with a real share-sheet target, a web app that installs like an app, and a WhatsApp bot, all on one library.

Private by design
